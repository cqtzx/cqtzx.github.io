Research interests

Low-rank matrix recovery
I use low-rank structure and spatial regularization to recover noisy images and other matrix data.
Structured sparse modeling
I use nonconvex regularization to model group sparsity and within-group smoothness and study the resulting recovery error.
Robust feature learning
I study robust image feature extraction and unsupervised domain adaptation under distribution shift.
